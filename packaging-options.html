Protection and handling first.

Backing Cards

Bulk Packing

Retail-Ready Packaging

The retail face is designed with the product.

For museum shops and cultural retail, tourism and souvenir ranges, stationery and education programs, promotional campaigns and licensed merchandise, packaging is part of what the buyer is selling.

Blister Packaging

Formed blister presentation for products that need a defined cavity and a visible retail face.

Display Boxes

Printed Sleeves and Wraps

Gift & Collection Packaging

Boxed Sets

Packaging Development Process

How the packaging direction is fixed.

Packaging decisions follow the product decision. These are the steps we work through with buyers.

  1. Product and channel reviewConstruction, quantity split, retail channel and destination market are reviewed together.
  2. Direction shortlistA packaging direction is shortlisted from the options on this page and matched to how the item must present.
  3. Artwork and print reviewWhere the packaging carries branding, the artwork is reviewed against the visible retail face.
  4. Insert and set fitInsert and multi-piece set composition are confirmed against the product and its box.
  5. Sample and approvalPackaging is reviewed with the approved physical sample before bulk production is released.

Standard MOQ starts from 200 pcs/sets in total per product construction and manufacturing process. Multiple designs may be combined, subject to project review. Sampling for custom packaging is confirmed per project.
