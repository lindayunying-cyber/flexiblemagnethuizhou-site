Our Story

Built on Magnetic Expertise. Focused on Flexible Materials and Finished Merchandise.

Flexible Magnet’s experience is rooted in the magnetic materials industry. As the business developed, our manufacturing operations evolved into two specialised areas: Flexible Magnet (Guangdong) Co., Ltd., headquartered in Guangzhou and focused primarily on NdFeB magnetic products, and Flexible Magnet (Huizhou) Co., Ltd., the Huizhou manufacturing company within the Flexible Magnet group, specialising in rubber magnetic materials and finished magnetic merchandise.

At our Huizhou operation, material knowledge forms the foundation of product development. Our understanding of flexible magnetic materials, print compatibility, converting and product construction helps us develop custom fridge magnets and magnetic merchandise that are practical to manufacture, consistent in production and ready for retail.

Today, the Huizhou team supports museum shops, tourism and destination retailers, stationery and educational brands, promotional programs and private-label collections. We help professional buyers connect artwork, materials, product structure, packaging and production planning—turning creative ideas into scalable magnetic products for international markets.

How We Work

From inquiry to delivery in 5 steps.

1

Inquiry

You send specifications, design or reference images, quantity range and destination market for project review.

2

Quote & Mockup

Pricing and artwork review are confirmed according to the product specification, quantity range, construction and packaging requirements.

3

Sample

Physical sampling typically takes 7–10 calendar days after artwork, product specifications, sample charges and other required details are confirmed. Timing may vary by product construction and packaging requirements.

4

Production

Standard bulk production typically takes 15–30 calendar days after all required approvals, deposit and complete production information are confirmed. Shipping time is not included.

5

Shipping

Shipping methods and trade terms are reviewed according to the destination, order requirements and agreed project terms. Export documentation is coordinated accordingly.
