Our Origin

Made for brands that need more than a product supplier.

Flexible Magnet (Huizhou) manufactures magnetic cultural and creative products for professional buyers—from museum retail and tourism programs to stationery, toys, promotions and private-label collections.

Our role is to bring the right material, process and production plan together, then carry a project through to finished goods and global delivery.

Our Factory

15,000 m² of Engineering-First Manufacturing.

Our Guangdong facility brings Heidelberg printing, CNC engraving, steel tooling and automated assembly together across 15,000 m². This lets us control the three essentials of magnetic product quality: material stability, print accuracy and bonding durability.

For premium souvenir sets and stationery, steel molds create clean, burr-free edges. For epoxy products, a controlled A:B = 2.5:1 resin ratio and 120-hour curing cycle help deliver a stable, clear finish designed to resist yellowing and shrinkage for up to five years.
