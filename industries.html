Whether you sell on Amazon, in K-12 channels, or D2C — magnetic stationery has to survive student hands and parent scrutiny. We build for both.

Common pain points we solve

  • EN71-3 / compliance failures on cheap competitors that get pulled from Amazon listings.

  • ✅ Bulk PE bag + master carton option for wholesalers who repackage downstream.
