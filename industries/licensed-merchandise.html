BUYER NEEDS / PROJECT CONTEXT

Approval and version control

Licensed programs rely on traceable approvals. Artwork versions, colour references, construction, pack copy and territory requirements should remain connected from the sampling brief through bulk-production release.

  • Licensees and authorized suppliers controlling approved artwork across several SKUs
  • Publishers and entertainment merchandise teams managing colour, version and pack approvals
  • Retail program managers coordinating assortments for territories or release windows
