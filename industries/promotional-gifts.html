BUYER NEEDS / PROJECT CONTEXT

Campaign approval planning

Campaign work is approval-led. The quotation basis, artwork owner, colour references, delivery window and packaging scope need to be visible before sampling so agency, end-brand and production decisions stay aligned.

  • Promotional agencies managing client approvals, campaign deadlines and presentation samples
  • Corporate procurement teams coordinating brand colours, messages and distribution quantities
  • Marketing distributors requiring a clear path from artwork review to labeled delivery
