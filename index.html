FAQ

Planning your custom magnetic product project

These are the core details buyers usually confirm before sampling and production.

What is your standard MOQ?

Standard MOQ starts from 200 pcs/sets in total per product construction and manufacturing process. Multiple designs may be combined, subject to project review.

Can multiple designs be combined?

Multiple designs may be combined within the standard MOQ, subject to artwork, printing layout, tooling, packaging and quantity allocation review.

How long does physical sampling take?

Physical sampling typically takes 7–10 calendar days after artwork, construction, sampling method, charges and applicable tooling requirements are confirmed.

How long does bulk production take?

Standard bulk production typically takes 15–30 calendar days after the required project information, approvals and deposit are confirmed. Shipping time is not included.

What information is required for a quotation?

Please share the product type, size, quantity, artwork or reference image, target market and packaging requirements.
