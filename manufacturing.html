Huizhou Manufacturing Facility

A 21,400 m² Independently Verified Facility.

Flexible Magnet (Huizhou) Co., Ltd. operates the group’s Huizhou manufacturing facility, specialising in rubber magnetic materials, custom fridge magnets and magnetic merchandise.

Across 21,400 m² of total facility floor space, our team supports material preparation, product construction, sampling, printing, finishing, assembly, packaging and delivery coordination for custom magnetic product programs.
