Our Process

Five stages. One manufacturing partner.

A clear OEM/ODM workflow for museum retail, stationery, tourism and promotional projects.

01

Design & Manufacturing Review

Send your artwork, reference image or product brief. We review shape, dimensions, print areas and material suitability before tooling or sampling so the production route is clear from the beginning.

  • Artwork, dimensions and packaging requirements reviewed together
  • Die-lines, cut paths and 3D construction planned in-house
  • Material and finish selected for the intended application
02

Material & Sample Validation

Sampling uses the intended material and process wherever possible, giving you a practical checkpoint for appearance, construction, magnet performance and packaging before bulk production.

  • Material-specific proofing for flat, epoxy, acrylic and 3D products
  • Construction and magnet performance reviewed before bulk production
  • Sampling scope and timing confirmed with your quotation
03

Precision Tooling & Production Planning

Process-specific tooling supports repeatable bulk production. For premium epoxy products and sets, steel molds create clean, burr-free edges that standard die-cutting cannot achieve.

  • Process-Specific Tooling: Tooling route confirmed against the approved construction
  • Steel Tooling: Precise blanking for a clean edge finish
  • Material Stability: Synthetic paper helps address delamination risk in stationery
04

Quality Review Against the Approved Sample

Before bulk release, the approved physical sample and specification become the quality reference for the run. Construction, finish and packaging are checked against that reference rather than left to assumption.

  • Layer-bonding checks to reduce delamination risk
  • Clear finish and shape stability reviewed before release
  • Documentation requirements scoped for the destination market
05

Export & Delivery Coordination

From our Guangdong factory to your nominated destination, we coordinate export documentation and logistics options around your project and delivery requirements.

  • Sea, air and express options reviewed per project
  • Export documentation and customs support
  • Air-cargo packaging requirements evaluated where applicable
